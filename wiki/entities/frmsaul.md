LessWrong author of [The Expensive-Loop Problem](../sources/the-expensive-loop-problem.md) (2026-10-13). Only the handle is given in the source; no affiliation or background is stated. Writes on AI capabilities and safety from an AI-R&D-evaluation angle (the essay draws heavily on PostTrainBench, an Amazon production-ML study and TasteVal).

Position: the gap between cheap-loop and expensive-loop competence is a central, under-discussed axis of AI capability — with implications for both usefulness (most high-value work is expensive-loop) and safety (strong cheap-loop, weak expensive-loop systems are reward-hacking risks).
