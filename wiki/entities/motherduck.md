# MotherDuck

Company hosting [DuckDB](duckdb.md) as a cloud warehouse (`md:` prefix; multi-tenancy, auth, backups). Publishes a technical blog with hands-on DuckDB write-ups and sponsors some third-party DuckDB content (e.g. the DuckLake maintenance piece). Co-founded by Jordan Tigani ([DuckDB's Agent Moment](../sources/duckdb-agent-moment.md)). Plans 2.0 support close to the DuckDB 2.0 release.

Treat its benchmarks as vendor-authored: [Source: Why DuckDB 2.0 Is Faster](../sources/2026-09-10-why-duckdb-2-is-faster.md); also [MotherDuck: Trustworthy AI Pipelines](../sources/motherduck-trustworthy-ai-pipelines.md) and [DuckDB for Agents](../concepts/duckdb-for-agents.md).
